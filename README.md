# THE ASCENT — V6.1

This iteration preserves the V6.0 assets, camera coordinates, StairMaster lettering, mountain and climber.

- The last summit story block settles at the bottom of the scene instead of scrolling above it.
- The final summit text and its shading stay visible until the contact section takes over; the summit story requires less scrolling overall.
- The scroll interval between the final StairMaster message and the mountain is compressed, without changing the camera positions or route.
- The native right-edge browser scrollbar is hidden. Scrolling itself is unaffected.

The archive places `index.html` at the root alongside `assets/`. Serve over HTTPS or a local HTTP server for Three.js modules and GLB assets.
